GymClock support

GymClock times a whole gym day: travel, workout, shower, travel home, and the meal after. It keeps everything on your phone.

Questions and problems

Email sashalawrence100@yahoo.com. Say which iPhone and iOS version you have and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

Where is my data? On your phone only. Deleting the app deletes the history. Use Export CSV in History to keep a copy.

The rest timer stopped buzzing when I locked the phone. Haptics only run while the app is open. Allow notifications when asked and GymClock sends an alert at the limit and at 30, 60 and 120 seconds over.

I bought GymClock Full on another phone. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase with the same Apple account.
