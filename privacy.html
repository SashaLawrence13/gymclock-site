GymClock privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026.

GymClock does not collect, store or send any personal data. There is no account, no sign in, no analytics and no advertising.

Your sessions, phases and settings are saved only on your device. The app has no network code of its own. If you export a CSV, it goes wherever you choose to share it.

The one-time purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. GymClock receives only a confirmation that the purchase is valid and never sees your payment details.

Notifications, if you allow them, are scheduled on the device for the rest timer and are not sent through any server.
